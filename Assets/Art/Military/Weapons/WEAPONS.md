# Weapons and optics

CC0 first-person weapon art next to the AK-47 (`AK47/`), plus two optics. Everything here is generated by
`Assets/Editor/FpsTools/WeaponImport.cs` (menu `Tools/FPS/Import Weapons`, or
`Unity -batchmode -quit -projectPath . -executeMethod WeaponImport.Apply`). The script is idempotent: assets are overwritten in
place, so GUIDs and any references to them stay valid. Sources and licences are in `../CREDITS.md`.

Nothing in `Assets/Scripts`, `Assets/Scenes` or the existing prefabs was changed; these are drop-in pieces.

## Conventions (same as the first-person AK)

- Per weapon: `<Name>/<Name>_Static.asset` (baked static mesh, muzzle tip at the mesh origin, +Z toward the muzzle, +Y up),
  `<Name>.mat` (Standard shader: albedo, normal map, metallic/smoothness, AO; textures at most 1024 px, normal maps flagged),
  `<Name>_View.prefab`. The Shotgun has an albedo texture only (no PBR maps in the source), so its material is a fixed
  metallic 0.15 / smoothness 0.3.
- Real-world scale. The AK's gun space is the player's `FpsGun_AK47` space: the node "AK47 Model" sits at
  `Anchor = (0, 0.11, 0.8521187)` with scale 171.42331, and the AK is 0.88 m long, so **1 m = k = 2.0075 gun units**.
  In every `_View.prefab` the child `<Name> Model` sits at `Anchor` with scale `k` (the mesh is in metres), so it replaces
  "AK47 Model" one to one.
- The `_View.prefab` root is at the origin with scale 1, everything on layer 9 (FPSGun), renderer settings like the AK's
  (the optic renderers have shadows off). Marker children
  (`Muzzle`, `Grip`, `Handguard`, `Rail`) are plain empties in that root space (gun space).
  `gun space = Anchor + k * (mesh-space metres)`.
- Marker meaning: `Muzzle` = barrel end (where `GunBarrelEnd` sits on the AK: use it for the tracer, flash and shot origin).
  `Grip` = right-hand position, `Handguard` = left-hand position (two-handed guns only), `Rail` = where an optic's `Mount` goes.
- To swap a gun: instantiate `<Name>_View.prefab` as a child of the gun root in place of "AK47 Model", move `GunBarrelEnd` to
  `Muzzle`, aim the IK hand targets at `Grip` / `Handguard`.

Notes:

- The Stein pack has no shotgun, so the pump shotgun comes from a second CC0 pack (3Dexter3D). The original is low-poly
  PS1 style with a single albedo; it is the least detailed of the five guns.
- The pack's pistol/SMG/sniper FBXs are skinned meshes whose bones are scaled 1/100, so the importer bakes them and fits
  the result to the real length in the table. The SMG and shotgun get a small bore shift/pitch correction so the barrel
  axis passes through the `Muzzle` marker at y = 0.11 like the AK.
- The Grip and Handguard positions are my best placement of the palm, not rig data: check them against the IK hand targets
  in the editor and nudge the markers in the prefab if a hand clips the model.
- AK rail: the AK has no `_View.prefab` (its model lives inside `FpsGun_AK47`). Its rail is the top cover of the receiver at
  mesh `(0, 0.024, -0.47)`, which in `FpsGun_AK47` gun space is `Anchor + k * rail = (0, 0.1582, -0.0914)`.
  Put an optic's `Mount` there.

## Optics

Both optics are procedural (cylinders, boxes, lathe), so no third-party model is involved. Prefabs:
`Optics/RedDot/RedDot_View.prefab` and `Optics/Scope4x/Scope4x_View.prefab`. Same conventions as the weapon views: +Z forward,
+Y up, root scale 1, child `<Name> Model` scaled by `k` (mesh in metres), layer 9.

| Optic | Look | Length x tube diameter | `Mount` (local) | `Eye` (local) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| RedDot | Aimpoint-style tube sight, 1x | 0.104 m x 0.033 m | (0, 0, 0): underside of the rail saddle | (0, 0.0683, 0.0594) | Lens quad `Lens` (faint cyan tint, 16% alpha) and `Dot` (red dot disc with a soft halo, unlit, alpha blended) at the objective end, centred on the sight axis. Eye is at the dot. |
| Scope4x | ACOG-style compact scope, 4x | 0.150 m x 0.035 m | (0, 0, 0) | (0, 0.0683, -0.1435) | `Objective` and `Ocular` lens discs (dark blue tint). Eye is at the ocular lens. Two turrets on top and side. No reticle: use a UI reticle or a 4x FOV in the aim camera. |

- The sight axis is 0.034 m (0.0683 gun units) above `Mount`, so for any gun the aim camera goes to
  `Rail + Eye` (both in the gun's view space) and looks along +Z. The bore is at y = 0.11.
- Mounting: add the optic prefab as a child of the weapon's view root and set its local position to the gun's `Rail`
  position (its `Mount` is the optic root origin, so no further offset is needed). For the AK, put it under `FpsGun_AK47`
  at the AK rail position above.
- Materials: `RedDot.mat` / `Scope4x.mat` are Standard (dark metal). `RedDot_Lens.mat`, `RedDot_Dot.mat` and
  `Scope4x_Lens.mat` use the built-in `Sprites/Default` shader (unlit, alpha blended), which works on Built-in RP and
  WebGL and is pulled into the build by the material reference. `RedDot_Dot.png` is a 64 px dot texture.
- Which guns: RedDot and Scope4x fit AK-47, SMG, Sniper and Shotgun (Shotgun with a red dot is the sensible pick).
  Pistol has a short Picatinny rail on the slide, so only the RedDot is realistic there; the importer still
  writes a `Rail` for it.

## Suggested gameplay stats

Tuned against the AK (`FpsGun`: damage 20, headshot x2.5, 0.2 s between bullets, range 100 m, mag 30, reserve 90, reload
2.2 s, player health 100). Damage per second is the same order for the full-auto guns so the choice is range and handling, not
raw power. The in-game fields are the ones on `FpsGun` / `ServerWeapon`.

| Weapon | Damage | Headshot x | Seconds between shots | Fire mode | Range (m) | Magazine | Reserve | Reload (s) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AK-47 (existing) | 20 | 2.5 | 0.20 | auto | 100 | 30 | 90 | 2.2 | about 100 dps, medium recoil |
| Pistol (1911) | 26 | 2.0 | 0.28 | semi | 60 | 12 | 48 | 1.6 | sidearm, low recoil, fastest to draw |
| SMG (MP5) | 12 | 2.0 | 0.11 | auto | 50 | 30 | 120 | 1.8 | about 110 dps, light recoil, strong hip fire |
| Sniper (M700) | 85 | 2.5 | 1.50 | bolt | 400 | 5 | 20 | 3.0 | body 85, head 212 (one shot), long bolt cycle, best with the 4x |
| Shotgun (pump) | 12 x 8 pellets | 1.5 | 0.90 | pump | 25 | 6 | 24 | 3.3 (0.55 s per shell) | up to 96 body at point blank, damage falls off with range, pump between shots |

Damage falloff suggestion for the shotgun: full pellets up to 8 m, linear to 25% at the range limit. Sniper bullets should be
a hitscan like the AK, the range alone makes it the long-range pick.

## Audio

Mono, 44.1 kHz Ogg Vorbis (q5), peak normalised to -1 dBFS, trimmed to the first shot, in `Assets/Audio/Weapons/<Name>/`:

| Weapon | Shot | Reload / action |
|---|---|---|
| Pistol | `Pistol_Shot.ogg` (0.9 s) | `Pistol_Reload.ogg` (1.6 s) |
| SMG | `SMG_Shot.ogg` (0.6 s) | `SMG_Reload.ogg` (1.6 s) |
| Sniper | `Sniper_Shot.ogg` (1.8 s) | `Sniper_Bolt.ogg` (0.5 s, pitched-down pump rack; there is no CC0 bolt sample) |
| Shotgun | `Shotgun_Shot.ogg` (1.4 s) | `Shotgun_Pump.ogg` (0.5 s) |

The Prepared SFX Library shots are close recordings with a tail, so keep the AudioSource spatial blend at 1 and a
max distance of 120+ m for the sniper. Shotgun reload is shell by shell: play a short insert click or reuse the pump clip.

## Rebuilding and previews

`Tools/FPS/Import Weapons` (or `-executeMethod WeaponImport.Apply`) regenerates every asset above from the FBXs and textures in
the weapon folders. `WeaponImport.Preview -previewDir <dir>` renders PNG previews (side view with a 10 cm grid, perspective,
optic close-ups, the sight view through each optic, and lineups of all weapons and optics beside the AK). Previews are not
stored in `Assets`.
